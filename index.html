02 — How it runs

Tablet to laser, in seconds

Guests order on our tablet station — it goes straight to the machine and gets engraved on the spot. No line, no clipboard, no downtime between orders — it's what makes us one of the most efficient custom live laser engravers you can book.

01

Guests order at the tablet

They pick the item and type their name or message on our order station — no line, no clipboard. We design the tablet screen around your event: your names, colors, and item list.

02

It goes straight to the laser

The order lands in our queue and is sent to the machine — no retyping, no mix-ups.

03

We engrave it in front of them

Minutes later they watch their piece get cut and walk away with it, still warm.
